# Study Plan

GARP reports successful candidates average **~240–275 hours** of preparation
for Part I. Two schedules are below — pick the one matching your runway, then
adapt. Both follow the same principles:

- **Order matters.** Books build on each other: Foundations gives context,
  Quant gives tools, Markets gives instruments, Valuation puts it together.
- **Questions beat re-reading.** Target at least 40% of total hours on
  practice questions. Reading feels productive; retrieval is what passes exams.
- **Weight by exam weight.** Books 3 and 4 are 60% of the exam — give them
  60% of your practice time.
- **Never lose calculator fluency.** Use your TI BA II Plus / HP 12C for
  every single computation from day one, even trivial ones.

## The error log (do not skip this)

Every question you get wrong, log four things:

1. The topic/chapter.
2. *Why* you got it wrong: concept gap, formula slip, calculator error, or
   misread question.
3. The correct reasoning, in one sentence, in your own words.
4. Tally by chapter — your top-5 chapters by error count are your final-week
   syllabus.

Most failed attempts come from candidates who did questions but never
systematically closed the gaps the questions revealed.

## Final 7 days

- One formula sheet per day, written out from memory, checked against the
  [Formula Sheets](formula-sheets/).
- GARP's **official practice exam** (free with registration — see
  [Resources](resources)) under timed conditions. It is the single best
  predictor of the real exam's style.
- Re-do every question in your error log.
- No new material in the last 48 hours. Sleep.
