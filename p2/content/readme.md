# 🎯 FRM® Part II — Complete Study Hub

> Everything you need to pass GARP's Financial Risk Manager **Part II** exam, built around the **2026 curriculum** — all **107 readings** across six books, with flashcards, interactive quizzes and full mock exams.

You cleared Part I. Part II is a different animal: same four hours, but **80 questions instead of 100**, and the questions are markedly more **applied and scenario-based** — less "recall the formula", more "a risk manager observes X, what should they conclude?".

Use the **sidebar** to jump to any reading; each chapter has **📖 Notes**, **🃏 Flashcards** and **📝 Quiz**. Your progress, quiz answers, spaced-repetition schedule and streak are saved in your browser, entirely separately from your Part I data.

## 📋 The exam at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Format | Computer-based test |
| Questions | **80** equally weighted multiple-choice questions |
| Duration | **4 hours** |
| Negative marking | **None** — never leave a question blank |
| 2026 windows | **16–19 May** · **7–8 August** · **21–25 November** |
| Calculators | TI BA II Plus (incl. Professional), HP 12C, HP 10B II, HP 10BII+, HP 20B |
| Scoring | GARP publishes **no** minimum passing score — results are relative to the candidate cohort |
| After passing | Part II must be passed within **4 years** of passing Part I; certification then needs **2 years** of relevant work experience |

{: .warning }
Any "you need ~51–53%" figure you see online is a **candidate-survey estimate, not a GARP number**. Treat it as folklore. Aim for 75%+ on practice and stop worrying about the cutoff.

## 📚 The six books and their exam weights

| Book | Topic | Weight | Readings | ≈ Questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Market Risk Measurement and Management | 20% | 18 | ~16 |
| 2 | Credit Risk Measurement and Management | 20% | 23 | ~16 |
| 3 | Operational Risk and Resilience | 20% | 24 | ~16 |
| 4 | Liquidity and Treasury Risk Measurement and Management | 15% | 17 | ~12 |
| 5 | Risk Management and Investment Management | 15% | 17 | ~12 |
| 6 | Current Issues in Financial Markets | 10% | 8 | ~8 |

The per-topic question counts are arithmetic from the weights — GARP does not guarantee an exact split, and the exam has **no labelled sections**; topics are interleaved.

{: .important }
Books 1–3 are **60% of the exam** between them. Book 3 (Operational Risk and Resilience) is the single biggest surprise for most Part I graduates: it is long, heavily qualitative, and full of Basel and regulatory-guidance readings that reward careful reading over calculation.

## ✅ What changed for 2026 — read this before reusing old notes

- **Book 5 (Risk Management and Investment Management) was overhauled: 11 → 17 readings.** Do **not** study this book from 2025 material. Private credit, private markets and several manager-diligence readings were added.
- **Book 4 (Liquidity and Treasury) shrank: 19 → 17 readings** — deletions only, nothing added.
- **Book 6 (Current Issues) was fully refreshed, as it is every single year** — 5 of the 8 readings are new for 2026. It now centres on **AI in capital markets**, **private credit**, **geopolitical risk**, **crypto/tokenisation** and **digital resilience**. Notably there is **no dedicated climate-risk reading** and **no SVB/Credit Suisse reading** this year, contrary to what many older guides assume.
- **Books 1, 2 and 3 are unchanged** in reading list for 2026 (Book 3 is now titled "Operational Risk and **Resilience**").
- Total readings rose from **104 → 107**.

## 🗓️ How to use this hub

1. **Read** a chapter's Notes, then flip its **Flashcards** and *rate each card* (Again / Hard / Good / Easy) — rated cards resurface on the right day in **🔁 Smart Flashcards**.
2. **Drill** the chapter **Quiz**: the easy tier builds fluency, the 🎯 exam-level tier is the one that actually looks like Part II. ⭐ star anything you want to revisit.
3. Do the **🎯 Daily Drill** daily — ten questions weighted like the real exam. Keep the 🔥 streak.
4. Your **error log keeps itself**: every miss lands in the **🧠 Review Hub** until you answer it correctly.
5. Final fortnight: both **Mock Exams** under timed conditions, clear the Review Hub, sweep your ⭐ bookmarks and drill the formula sheets.

## ⏱️ Suggested schedule

Part II rewards depth over breadth-cramming; GARP-surveyed candidates average **~240–275 hours** again.

**16 weeks (~15 hrs/wk):** Wks 1–3 Book 1 · 4–6 Book 2 · 7–9 Book 3 · 10–11 Book 4 · 12–13 Book 5 · 14 Book 6 (leave it late — it is short and current) · 15 Mock 1 + error review · 16 Mock 2, formula sheets, weak-topic re-drill.

**10 weeks (~25 hrs/wk):** Wk 1–2 Book 1 · 3–4 Book 2 · 5–6 Book 3 · 7 Book 4 · 8 Book 5 + Book 6 · 9 Mock 1 + Review Hub · 10 Mock 2 + final sweep.
