# Exam-Day Guide — FRM Part II

## Registration and logistics

- Register through your GARP candidate portal at [garp.org](https://www.garp.org/frm). Early registration is materially cheaper than standard.
- **2026 Part II windows:** 16–19 May · 7–8 August · 21–25 November. The August window offers Part I and Part II on the same weekend, including a same-day both-parts option.
- Delivered by computer at PSI test centres (ATA in China Mainland, Hong Kong SAR and Thailand).
- You must pass Part II within **four years** of passing Part I, then evidence **two years** of relevant professional experience to be certified.

## Format and what it implies

- **80 questions, 4 hours → ~3 minutes per question.** Definition-style questions take under a minute; bank that time for the multi-step counterparty-risk and term-structure items.
- **No negative marking.** Answer everything. In the last two minutes, fill every blank.
- **No labelled sections** — topics are interleaved, so you cannot "save Book 5 for last".
- **No formula sheet is provided.** Everything in the Reference section must be in your head.

{: .warning }
GARP publishes no minimum passing score. Widely-quoted "51–53%" numbers are candidate-survey estimates, not policy. Don't calibrate your effort to a rumour — target 75%+ on practice questions.

## Calculator

Only these are permitted: **TI BA II Plus** (incl. Professional), **HP 12C** (incl. Platinum / Anniversary), **HP 10B II**, **HP 10BII+**, **HP 20B**.

Part II leans on your calculator less than Part I, but when it does the work is heavier. Be automatic with:

| Skill | Where it bites in Part II |
|---|---|
| TVM keys, `P/Y = 1`, `CLR TVM` | Bond/loan cash flows, funding costs |
| `y^x`, `LN`, `e^x` | Default intensity, hazard rates, continuous discounting |
| Cash-flow worksheet (NPV/IRR) | Structured-credit and expected-exposure profiles |
| STAT / regression | Hedge ratios, factor regressions, PCA framing |
| Memory stores | Multi-step counterparty and VaR chains |

{: .tip }
Set decimals to 4+ (`2ND → FORMAT`). Part II's multi-step questions punish mid-calculation rounding: the distractors are often built from exactly that error.

## Answering strategy

1. **Three passes.** Pass 1: everything under 2 minutes. Pass 2: flagged calculations. Pass 3: sanity-check and fill blanks.
2. **Read the final sentence first** on long scenario stems — it tells you what to extract from the story.
3. **Watch the qualifiers**: *most likely*, *least likely*, *best describes*, *EXCEPT*. Part II uses these far more than Part I, and misreading one costs the mark outright.
4. **Name the measure before you compute.** Many Part II traps swap two related quantities (CVA vs DVA, expected exposure vs PFE, LCR vs NSFR, VaR vs ES). Identify which is asked, then calculate.
5. **A clean round number in a multi-step problem is usually the trap** — it is what you get by stopping a step early.

## Exam-day checklist

- Passport-grade ID matching your registration name exactly.
- Approved calculator + spare batteries (memory may be inspected/cleared).
- Arrive 30+ minutes early for check-in.
- Scratch boards are provided; you cannot bring your own paper.
- Pacing marker: at the 2-hour mark you should be at or past question 40.
