# Resources — FRM Part II

Everything here is legitimately free or already included in your exam fee. The notes in this app are original, written to the published 2026 Part II curriculum — a companion to the official readings, not a replacement.

{: .important }
Part II's Book 6 (Current Issues) is **replaced every year**, and several of its readings are freely downloadable primary sources — IMF Global Financial Stability Report chapters, FSB papers, BIS Quarterly Review articles. Read those originals; they are short, and GARP tests them closely.

## Free primary sources worth reading directly

- **IMF** — Global Financial Stability Report chapters (AI in capital markets; geopolitical risk) — imf.org
- **FSB** — financial stability implications of AI — fsb.org
- **BIS** — Quarterly Review articles (private credit), Annual Economic Report, and the Basel framework texts — bis.org
- **Basel Committee** — Basel III finalisation and the high-level summary — bis.org/bcbs

Third-party chapter indexes go stale fast, and for 2026 several are still showing the 2025 Part II lists — Book 5 in particular grew from 11 to 17 readings. Always reconcile any outside material against your GARP study guide before trusting its scope.

## A note on copyrighted material

GARP's books and commercial providers' notes are copyrighted. Sites offering free PDFs of them are pirated and usually outdated — a 2024 Part II PDF would teach you a Book 5 that no longer exists. The official eBooks are already covered by your registration fee.
