# Study Plan — FRM Part II

Part I taught you the vocabulary of risk. Part II tests whether you can **use** it. The questions are longer, more scenario-driven, and often ask which conclusion a risk manager should draw rather than which number falls out of a formula.

## What is different from Part I

| | Part I | Part II |
|---|---|---|
| Questions | 100 | **80** |
| Time per question | ~2.4 min | **~3.0 min** |
| Style | Definitions + single-step calculations | Applied scenarios, multi-step, judgement |
| Heaviest books | Markets, Valuation (60%) | Market, Credit, Operational (60%) |
| Volatile content | None | **Book 6 changes every year** |

{: .warning }
The classic Part I graduate's mistake is under-rating **Book 3 (Operational Risk and Resilience)**. It is 24 readings, 20% of the exam, and largely qualitative — Basel, model risk, cyber, outsourcing, capital planning. You cannot cram it the night before; it rewards steady reading and the flashcards.

## 10-week plan (~25 hrs/week)

Wk 1–2 Book 1 · 3–4 Book 2 · 5–6 Book 3 · 7 Book 4 · 8 Book 5 + Book 6 · 9 Mock 1 + Review Hub · 10 Mock 2 + final sweep.

## The loop that actually moves the needle

1. **Notes → Flashcards (rate them) → Quiz.** Rating is what schedules your revision; skipping it wastes the whole spaced-repetition engine.
2. **Daily Drill every day.** Ten exam-weighted questions. The streak is the point — consistency beats weekend marathons.
3. **Review Hub until empty.** Every question you miss anywhere returns there until you get it right. That is your error log, and it maintains itself.
4. **Two mocks, spaced.** One at ~80% through the syllabus to find the holes; one in the final week to rehearse pacing.

{: .tip }
Pacing rehearsal matters more in Part II than Part I. 80 questions in 240 minutes is 3 minutes each, but the scenario questions can run 4–5 minutes. Practise **flagging and moving on** — the mock runner's timer is there for exactly this.

## Final 7 days

- One formula sheet per day, written from memory, checked against the Reference section.
- Re-do every question in the **Review Hub** and every ⭐ bookmark.
- Skim **Book 6 (Current Issues)** twice — it is only 8 readings and ~8 marks, the cheapest points on the paper.
- No new material in the last 48 hours. Sleep.
