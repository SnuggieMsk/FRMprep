# Book 4: Valuation and Risk Models

**Exam weight: 30% (~30 questions).** Where everything converges: VaR and
expected shortfall, volatility estimation, credit ratings and credit risk,
country risk, operational risk, stress testing, bond valuation and the term
structure, duration/convexity hedging, binomial trees, Black-Scholes-Merton,
and the Greeks.

{: .tip }
This book produces the exam's hardest calculation chains (e.g., two-step
binomial trees, duration+convexity price changes, 99% 10-day VaR scaling).
They are also the most *standardized* — drill them until they're muscle
memory and they become reliable points.
