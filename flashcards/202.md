**Q1.** Distinguish a PMF from a PDF.
> **A:** PMF gives $$f(x) = P(X = x)$$ for discrete variables; PDF gives density (not probability) for continuous variables, where $$P(X = x) = 0$$ and probability comes from integrating: $$P(a \le X \le b) = \int_a^b f(x)\,dx$$.

**Q2.** Can a PDF value exceed 1?
> **A:** Yes. Only probabilities (areas under the PDF) must lie in $$[0,1]$$. E.g., a uniform on $$[0, 0.5]$$ has $$f(x) = 2$$.

**Q3.** What are the properties of a CDF $$F(x) = P(X \le x)$$?
> **A:** Nondecreasing, runs from 0 to 1, works for discrete and continuous variables; for continuous variables $$f(x) = F'(x)$$.

**Q4.** State linearity of expectation, including the dependence caveat.
> **A:** $$E[aX + b] = aE[X] + b$$ and $$E[X + Y] = E[X] + E[Y]$$ — the sum rule holds always, even when $$X, Y$$ are dependent.

**Q5.** Give the computational formula for variance.
> **A:** $$\sigma^2 = E[(X-\mu)^2] = E[X^2] - (E[X])^2$$

**Q6.** Define skewness and kurtosis.
> **A:** Skewness $$= \dfrac{E[(X-\mu)^3]}{\sigma^3}$$ (asymmetry); kurtosis $$= \dfrac{E[(X-\mu)^4]}{\sigma^4}$$ (tail heaviness).

**Q7.** What is excess kurtosis, and what does a positive value mean?
> **A:** Excess kurtosis $$=$$ kurtosis $$- 3$$ (normal kurtosis is 3). Positive (leptokurtic) means fatter-than-normal tails — the empirical norm for returns.

**Q8.** For $$Y = aX + b$$, how do the mean, variance, and standard deviation transform?
> **A:** Mean $$= a\mu + b$$; variance $$= a^2\sigma^2$$; standard deviation $$= \lvert a\rvert\sigma$$.

**Q9.** For $$Y = aX + b$$, how do skewness and kurtosis transform?
> **A:** Kurtosis is unchanged. Skewness is unchanged if $$a > 0$$; its sign flips if $$a < 0$$. The additive constant $$b$$ affects none of them.

**Q10.** Define the quantile function and the interquartile range.
> **A:** $$Q(\alpha) = F^{-1}(\alpha)$$, the value below which $$X$$ falls with probability $$\alpha$$; $$\text{IQR} = Q(0.75) - Q(0.25)$$.

**Q11.** Why are quantile-based measures important for risk?
> **A:** They are robust to outliers and exist even when moments do not (e.g., Cauchy); VaR is precisely a quantile of the loss distribution.

**Q12.** $$X$$ has $$E[X] = 2$$ and $$E[X^2] = 13$$. Find the standard deviation.
> **A:** $$\sigma^2 = 13 - 2^2 = 9$$, so $$\sigma = 3$$.

**Q13.** State Jensen's inequality for a convex function $$g$$.
> **A:** $$E[g(X)] \ge g(E[X])$$; in general $$E[g(X)] \ne g(E[X])$$ for nonlinear $$g$$.
