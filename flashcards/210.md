**Q1.** State the three requirements for covariance stationarity.
> **A:** (1) constant mean $$E[Y_t] = \mu$$; (2) constant finite variance $$\mathrm{Var}(Y_t) = \gamma_0$$; (3) autocovariance $$\mathrm{Cov}(Y_t, Y_{t-h}) = \gamma_h$$ depends only on lag $$h$$, not on $$t$$.

**Q2.** Define the autocorrelation function (ACF) and the partial autocorrelation (PACF).
> **A:** $$\rho_h = \gamma_h/\gamma_0$$ (with $$\rho_0 = 1$$). PACF at lag $$h$$ = correlation of $$Y_t$$ and $$Y_{t-h}$$ after controlling for intermediate lags (the coefficient on $$Y_{t-h}$$ in an AR($$h$$) regression).

**Q3.** Define white noise and distinguish independent and Gaussian white noise.
> **A:** $$E[\epsilon_t]=0$$, $$\mathrm{Var}=\sigma^2$$, $$\rho_h = 0$$ for $$h\ne 0$$ (uncorrelated, not necessarily independent). Independent: additionally iid. Gaussian: iid $$N(0,\sigma^2)$$.

**Q4.** State the AR(1) model, its stationarity condition, and its moments.
> **A:** $$Y_t = \delta + \phi Y_{t-1} + \epsilon_t$$, stationary if $$\lvert\phi\rvert < 1$$. $$E[Y_t] = \dfrac{\delta}{1-\phi}$$, $$\mathrm{Var}(Y_t) = \dfrac{\sigma^2}{1-\phi^2}$$, $$\rho_h = \phi^h$$.

**Q5.** What is the mean-reverting level of an AR(1), and what is the classic trap?
> **A:** Long-run mean $$\dfrac{\delta}{1-\phi}$$, NOT $$\delta$$. With $$\phi = 0.95$$, $$\delta = 0.2$$ gives mean 4.0. If $$\phi \ge 1$$ there is no mean reversion at all.

**Q6.** State the MA(1) model and its moments.
> **A:** $$Y_t = \mu + \theta\epsilon_{t-1} + \epsilon_t$$. $$E[Y_t] = \mu$$, $$\mathrm{Var} = \sigma^2(1+\theta^2)$$, $$\rho_1 = \dfrac{\theta}{1+\theta^2}$$, $$\rho_h = 0$$ for $$h \ge 2$$. Stationary for any $$\theta$$.

**Q7.** Give the ACF/PACF identification signatures for AR, MA, and ARMA.
> **A:** AR($$p$$): ACF decays gradually, PACF cuts off after lag $$p$$. MA($$q$$): ACF cuts off after lag $$q$$, PACF decays. ARMA: both decay gradually.

**Q8.** Give the AR(1) h-step forecast formula.
> **A:** $$\hat Y_{t+h} = \dfrac{\delta}{1-\phi} + \phi^h\left(Y_t - \dfrac{\delta}{1-\phi}\right)$$ — converges geometrically to the mean-reversion level.

**Q9.** AR(1) with $$\phi = 0.6$$, mean 10, current value 16. Find the 2-step-ahead forecast.
> **A:** $$\hat Y_{t+2} = 10 + 0.6^2(16-10) = 10 + 0.36(6) = 12.16$$.

**Q10.** Interest-rate AR(1): $$r_t = 0.6 + 0.85\,r_{t-1} + \epsilon_t$$, current $$r_t = 5.0\%$$. Find the mean-reversion level and 1-step forecast.
> **A:** Mean $$= 0.6/(1-0.85) = 4.0\%$$; $$\hat r_{t+1} = 0.6 + 0.85(5.0) = 4.85\%$$.

**Q11.** State the Box–Pierce and Ljung–Box Q-statistics and their null.
> **A:** $$Q_{BP} = T\sum_{h=1}^m \hat\rho_h^2$$; $$Q_{LB} = T(T+2)\sum_{h=1}^m \dfrac{\hat\rho_h^2}{T-h}$$ (better in small samples). Both $$\sim \chi^2_m$$ under $$H_0$$ (white noise); reject for large values.

**Q12.** What is the significance band for a single sample autocorrelation under white noise?
> **A:** SE $$= 1/\sqrt{T}$$, so $$\hat\rho_h$$ outside $$\pm 1.96/\sqrt{T}$$ is significant.

**Q13.** Residuals: $$T=100$$, $$\hat\rho_1=0.20$$, $$\hat\rho_2=0.10$$, $$\hat\rho_3=0.05$$, $$m=3$$. Compute $$Q_{LB}$$ (crit $$\chi^2_3 = 7.81$$).
> **A:** $$Q_{LB} = 10200(0.04/99 + 0.01/98 + 0.0025/97) = 10200(0.000532) = 5.42 < 7.81$$ → fail to reject; consistent with white noise.

**Q14.** How does the lag operator express stationarity for an AR model?
> **A:** $$LY_t = Y_{t-1}$$; AR(1) becomes $$(1-\phi L)Y_t = \delta + \epsilon_t$$. Stationary when all roots of the lag polynomial lie outside the unit circle (for AR(1): $$\lvert\phi\rvert < 1$$).
