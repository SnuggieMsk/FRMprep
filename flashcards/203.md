**Q1.** Give the mean and variance of a Bernoulli($$p$$) and a Binomial($$n,p$$).
> **A:** Bernoulli: mean $$p$$, variance $$p(1-p)$$. Binomial: mean $$np$$, variance $$np(1-p)$$.

**Q2.** State the binomial probability formula.
> **A:** $$P(X = k) = \binom{n}{k} p^k (1-p)^{n-k}$$

**Q3.** State the Poisson PMF and its signature moment fact.
> **A:** $$P(X = k) = \dfrac{\lambda^k e^{-\lambda}}{k!}$$; mean $$=$$ variance $$= \lambda$$. Counts over disjoint periods add (monthly $$\lambda$$ → annual $$12\lambda$$).

**Q4.** Give the mean and variance of a continuous Uniform($$a,b$$).
> **A:** Mean $$= \dfrac{a+b}{2}$$; variance $$= \dfrac{(b-a)^2}{12}$$.

**Q5.** Memorize the normal tail cutoffs: two-tailed 95%, 99%; one-tailed 5%, 1%.
> **A:** Two-tailed: $$\pm 1.96$$ (95%), $$\pm 2.576$$ (99%). One-tailed: 1.645 beyond 5%, 2.326 beyond 1%. Also $$\pm 1.645$$ holds 90%.

**Q6.** Returns are normal but prices are what, and why?
> **A:** Lognormal: if $$\ln X$$ is normal, $$X$$ is lognormal — bounded below by zero and right-skewed, so prices can never go negative. Mean $$= e^{\mu + \sigma^2/2}$$.

**Q7.** How is the chi-squared distribution constructed, and what are its moments?
> **A:** $$\chi^2_k = \sum_{i=1}^k Z_i^2$$ of independent standard normals; mean $$= k$$, variance $$= 2k$$; right-skewed, positive support.

**Q8.** How is Student's t built, and how do its tails and critical values compare to the normal?
> **A:** $$t = \dfrac{Z}{\sqrt{W/\nu}}$$ with $$W \sim \chi^2_\nu$$. Fatter tails than normal, so t critical values are larger (intervals widen); variance $$= \dfrac{\nu}{\nu-2} > 1$$; converges to normal as $$\nu \to \infty$$.

**Q9.** How is the F-distribution defined, and how does it relate to t?
> **A:** Ratio of two independent chi-squareds each divided by its d.o.f.; right-skewed, positive. Note $$t_\nu^2 = F_{1,\nu}$$.

**Q10.** When does the Poisson approximate the binomial?
> **A:** When $$n$$ is large and $$p$$ small, with $$\lambda = np$$.

**Q11.** What is a mixture distribution and its PDF?
> **A:** Draws from one of several components with weights $$w_i$$ ($$\sum w_i = 1$$): $$f(x) = \sum_i w_i f_i(x)$$; mean is the weighted mean of component means.

**Q12.** What shape feature does a mixture of two normals with equal means but different variances produce?
> **A:** Excess kurtosis (fat tails) — symmetric but leptokurtic. Different means add skewness or bimodality.

**Q13.** A portfolio holds 10 independent loans each with 4% default probability. Probability of at least one default?
> **A:** $$1 - 0.96^{10} = 1 - 0.6648 = 0.3352$$

**Q14.** Outages are Poisson with $$\lambda = 2$$ per quarter. Find $$P(X = 0)$$.
> **A:** $$P(X = 0) = e^{-2} = 0.1353$$
