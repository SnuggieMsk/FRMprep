**Exam weight: 20% (~20 questions).** The conceptual backbone of the program:
what risk is, how firms govern and transfer it, the classical portfolio
theory toolkit (CAPM, APT, performance measures), what history teaches
(financial disasters, the 2007–09 crisis), and the GARP Code of Conduct.

Mostly qualitative, with calculations concentrated in Chapters 5–7
(CAPM, multifactor models, risk-adjusted performance ratios).

{: .tip }
Foundations questions are the fastest points on the exam for a prepared
candidate. The disasters chapter (Ch 9) and the Code of Conduct (Ch 11) are
near-guaranteed question sources and pure memory — know them cold.
