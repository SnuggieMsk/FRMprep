## The exam at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Format | Computer-based test (CBT) |
| Questions | 100 equally weighted multiple-choice questions |
| Duration | 4 hours |
| Exam windows (2026) | May, August, and November |
| Passing | No fixed pass mark — scored relative to candidates; results in ~6 weeks |
| Negative marking | None — **never leave a question blank** |
| Calculators allowed | TI BA II Plus (incl. Professional), HP 12C, HP 10B II / 10B II+, HP 20B |

{: .important }
Always verify dates, fees, and policies on the official site:
[garp.org/frm](https://www.garp.org/frm). This site is an independent study
companion built around GARP's published 2026 learning objectives.

## The four books

The 2026 Part I curriculum is unchanged in structure from 2025 — 62 chapters
across four topic areas. Weights below are GARP's official exam weights.

| Book | Topic | Weight | Chapters | ≈ Questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Book 1](book-1/) | Foundations of Risk Management | 20% | 11 | ~20 |
| [Book 2](book-2/) | Quantitative Analysis | 20% | 15 | ~20 |
| [Book 3](book-3/) | Financial Markets and Products | 30% | 20 | ~30 |
| [Book 4](book-4/) | Valuation and Risk Models | 30% | 16 | ~30 |

{: .tip }
Books 3 and 4 carry 60% of the exam and are the most calculation-heavy. If
your time is constrained, weight your practice toward them — but Foundations
and Quant contain "free marks" for candidates who know the definitions and
standard formulas cold.

## What's new for 2026

Nothing structural. GARP kept all 62 Part I chapters; the only changes are
reworded learning objectives in **Hypothesis Testing** and **Stationary Time
Series** (Book 2), shifting emphasis from definitions toward *applied
calculation and interpretation*. Expect slightly more numerical questions in
those areas — the notes and question bank here reflect that.

## How to use this site

1. Read the [Study Plan](study-plan) and pick the schedule that fits your
   exam window.
2. Work through the books in order (1 → 4); each chapter ends with a quick
   quiz — don't skip it.
3. After each book, do the book's question bank in [Practice](practice/).
4. In the final two weeks: formula sheets daily, the mock exam under timed
   conditions, then re-drill your weak areas.
5. Use the search bar (top of page) to find any concept or formula instantly.
