# Book 3: Financial Markets and Products

**Exam weight: 30% (~30 questions).** The largest book: financial
institutions, derivatives markets and clearing, forwards, futures, swaps,
options and option strategies, interest rates, bonds, mortgages, and FX and
commodity markets. Heavily computational — pricing, hedging, and payoff
questions dominate.

{: .tip }
The hedging chapters (8, 19) and pricing chapters (10, 20) are perennial
multi-question sources. Master cost-of-carry and the swap-as-bond-portfolio
decomposition and a large fraction of this book's questions become mechanical.
