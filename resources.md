# Resources

Every item here is **legitimately free / publicly available**. The notes on
this site are original and aligned to GARP's published learning objectives —
they are a study companion, not a replacement for the official curriculum.

## Official GARP materials (use all of these)

| Resource | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| FRM Study Guide | Official list of all 2026 readings per book | [garp.org/landing/frm-study-guide](https://www.garp.org/landing/frm-study-guide) |
| FRM Learning Objectives | The canonical, testable LO list for every chapter — the exam is written from this | [garp.org/landing/frm-learning-objectives](https://www.garp.org/landing/frm-learning-objectives) |
| Official Practice Exams | Free with candidate registration, in your GARP candidate portal | [garp.org/frm/study-materials](https://www.garp.org/frm/study-materials) |
| FRM Candidate Guide | Policies, scheduling, ID rules, calculator policy | [garp.org/frm/program-exams](https://www.garp.org/frm/program-exams) |
| GARP Code of Conduct | Directly tested in Book 1, Chapter 11 — read the primary document | [garp.org](https://www.garp.org/) (search "Code of Conduct") |
| Official eBooks | The full curriculum books — included with exam registration via GARP's reading platform | GARP candidate portal |

{: .important }
After you register, download the **Learning Objectives** PDF and keep it
beside these notes. Tick off each LO as you master it — the exam cannot ask
anything outside that document.

## Communities

- r/FRM on Reddit — active candidate community, exam debriefs each window.
- Bionic Turtle forums — best place to get a specific concept question answered.

## A note on copyrighted material

GARP's books and commercial prep providers' notes (Schweser, etc.) are
copyrighted. Sites offering "free" PDFs of them are pirated, frequently
outdated (a 2019 Schweser PDF will quietly teach you a retired curriculum),
and a bad trade. The official eBooks are already included in your exam
registration fee.
