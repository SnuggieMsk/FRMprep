# Practice

Retrieval practice is what passes this exam. Work in this order:

1. **Chapter quizzes** — at the end of every chapter's notes (Books 1–4).
2. **Book question banks** (here) — after finishing each book. Exam-style
   MCQs with four choices and fully worked explanations.
3. **Full mock exam** (here) — 100 questions weighted exactly like the real
   exam (20/20/30/30). Sit it in one 4-hour timed block, no notes, approved
   calculator only. Then grade it with the answer key and build your error log.
4. **GARP's official practice exam** — free in your candidate portal (see
   [Resources](../resources)) — in the final week.

| Score on the mock | Reading |
|---|---|
| 75%+ | On track — keep drilling weak chapters |
| 60–74% | Passable range but not safe — error log + targeted re-study |
| <60% | Re-study the two worst books before re-attempting questions |
